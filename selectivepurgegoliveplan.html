2. What changes between Preview and GA

There is one change in behaviour. Nothing else is different.

In Preview

Records marked for purge can be found by using Search.

Search: available
→

At GA

Search is not available for records marked for purge. These records are shown only in Ops Insight.

Search: not available Ops Insight: available

3. The three toggles

Think of each toggle as one switch that controls a group of features.

Toggle A

Main purge switch

Controls marking records for purge from each entry point, marking Source records, seeing marked records in Ops Insight, the Reorder Purge Job UI, invoking the Purge Job, and changing the BID of marked records.

Toggle B

Search visibility switch

Controls whether records marked for purge can be seen in Search. It is on during Preview. What happens to it at GA will be discussed and decided separately.

Toggle C

Master records and reinstate switch

Controls two things: the ability to mark Master records for purge, and the ability to reinstate records. While it is off, Master records cannot be marked, and neither Source nor Master records can be reinstated. It stays off in both Preview and GA.

Good to know
  • Internal only This toggle will not be communicated to GCS.
  • It is used by our own teams only and is not part of the customer or GCS messaging.
  • Keep it off in Preview and at GA. Do not enable it for any customer.

4. Go-live steps

Four simple stages. Do them in order.

1

Preview

Now
  • Turn Toggle A on for IFI, API, Business UI and Source records.
  • Turn Toggle B on so marked records can be seen in Search.
  • Keep Ingress and Ops Insight hidden. Keep Toggle C off.
2

Get ready for GA

Before the GA date
  • Collect Preview feedback and fix any open issues.
  • Prepare the customer message about the change in behaviour (see step 3).
  • Check in a test org that marked records show in Ops Insight and not in Search.
3

Go live (GA)

GA day
  • Enable Toggle A for all customers.
  • Search no longer shows records marked for purge.
  • Leave Toggle C off.
  • Tell customers about the change: once records are marked for purge, they can see the details in Ops Insight and not under Search.
4

After GA

First days after go-live
  • Check that marking records works from Ingress, IFI, API and Business UI.
  • Check that marked records appear in Ops Insight.
  • Watch for errors and customer questions about where to find marked records.
